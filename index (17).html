Questions

Frequently Asked

Does the science actually support solfeggio frequencies?
Honestly: evidence that specific solfeggio frequencies have special physical or healing effects is weak. QUANTRA uses them as part of a meditative tradition — as a focus for attention, alongside paced breathing, which is better studied for relaxation. We make no medical claims, and QUANTRA isn't a substitute for professional care.
How is Claude Edition different from just talking to ChatGPT?
Claude here is given a supportive, reflective tone and your name, intention and chat so far, and it sits alongside the tones, chakras and journal in one practice. It's a general-purpose AI with those instructions — not a licensed healer or therapist, and it can be wrong.
What if I don't believe in chakras or spirituality?
That's fine. Slow breathing is a well-studied way to relax, and the tones and geometry are optional aids. You can use QUANTRA as a secular calm practice.
How much time does the daily practice take?
The minimum effective practice is 10 minutes — a frequency tone with coherence breathing. Most members spend 20–30 minutes when they include journaling and oracle. The Claude Edition dialogue is usually 5–10 minutes of conversation. You choose your depth each day.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. Paid plans are billed through Stripe and can be cancelled at any time; you won't be charged for the next period.
Is my data private? Does Claude store my conversations?
Your messages are sent to Claude (Anthropic's AI) to generate replies. Chat history is kept only for your current session. Don't share information you wouldn't want an AI service to process.
